- Designed PulseRide with my SHAD team: a rickshaw-inspired electric three-wheeler for low-occupancy rides in downtown Vancouver. I was the team engineer and owned the energy efficiency and the CAD.
- Calculated the RPM band the motor needed at downtown speeds, then built a working mini-prototype from CAD-modelled, 3D-printed parts and custom circuit boards. When the gearbox housing flexed under vibration and pulled the gears out of alignment, I redesigned it, and learned KiCad to clean up the PCB layout.
- Our business plan estimated about 6.3 kWh per 100 km for the full-size vehicle, roughly 2.6 times more energy-efficient than a Tesla Model 3. The prototype won Best Prototype.
