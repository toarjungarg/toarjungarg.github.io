- Founded a charity that gets excess Halloween candy to children in developing countries. It started with me giving away my own leftover candy on family trips.
- Grew it from door-to-door drives into a donation-box network and pickup service across the GTA, with international distributions every year. 2,000+ candy bags donated so far.
- Led a 6-person executive team through a last-minute crisis when a partner food bank pulled out with two days' notice: set criteria for new recipients, ran a time-boxed emergency meeting, and coordinated a new international donation.
- Still run it part-time from Waterloo.
